# Oderlads

## Only do what I'm told

Do not create, rename, move, or delete files, and do not start building, unless
I directly ask for it.

Discussing an idea is not permission to act on it. Me being vague or positive
about something ("yeah, that sounds good") is not permission either. Wait until
I tell you to do it.

## DESIGN.md contains only what I said

DESIGN.md is a record of *my* decisions and the ideas I actually commented on.

Never add:
- ideas I did not comment on
- open questions you thought of
- your own proposals, suggestions, or recommendations
- bookkeeping about files or your own actions

If you raise something and I don't pick it up, it does not go in the document.

Raise your suggestions in conversation instead. They reach the document only if
I engage with them.

**Why this matters:**

1. When I come back to the doc later, I can't tell my ideas from yours. That
   makes the record useless.
2. When I ask you to build from the doc, anything you slipped in becomes a
   feature I never asked for.
3. I'm often still working out what the idea is. A doc full of your speculation
   anchors us both to decisions I never made.
4. Everything in the doc becomes context that steers you. More of your material
   means more of your assumptions in the result.

Keep it short. Shorter is better than complete.
